Acme Retail Pvt Ltd - FY2025 Annual Summary (sample document)

1. Financial highlights
Total revenue for FY2025 grew 14 percent year over year, driven mainly by the Electronics category and the Online sales channel. The North region remained the largest market, contributing roughly 30 percent of revenue, while the West region was the smallest but fastest growing.

2. Operations
The company opened two new fulfilment centres in Pune and Chennai, reducing average delivery time from 5 days to 3 days. Return rates stayed below 4 percent across all categories.

3. Discount policy
Discounts above 10 percent require written approval from the regional manager. Discounts above 15 percent are not permitted except during the annual clearance sale in January. Enterprise customers may receive contract pricing negotiated separately.

4. Risks
Key risks include dependence on a small number of suppliers for laptops and phones, currency fluctuation on imported components, and slower demand for Furniture during the second half of the year.

5. Outlook
Management expects revenue growth of 10 to 12 percent in FY2026 and plans to expand the Software category with new cloud subscription plans.
